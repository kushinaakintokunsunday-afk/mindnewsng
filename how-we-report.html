How We Report

Mindnewsng is a Nigerian digital newspaper. This page explains how we gather, verify, correct and publish news.

Sourcing

We draw on reporting from established Nigerian and international news organisations, official statements, court filings, government releases and verified eyewitness accounts. Every article carries a source line naming the originating outlet or organisation.

Verification

Before publication we check that the core facts (who, what, when, where) are supported by at least one named source. Where a claim cannot be verified, we say so. We do not publish rumours as fact.

Bylines

Articles compiled by our editorial desk carry the byline "Mindnewsng Desk" with a source line. Articles by named contributors carry their byline and a link to their author page.

Corrections

We correct errors promptly. See our Corrections Policy. To report an error, email editor@mindnewsng.com.

Independence

Our newsroom is independent. Sponsored content is always labelled "Sponsored" and kept separate from editorial. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Contact

Email: editor@mindnewsng.com. See also our About and Contact pages.
