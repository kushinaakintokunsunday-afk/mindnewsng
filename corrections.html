Corrections Policy

We aim for accuracy. When we get something wrong, we correct it.

How we correct

Minor errors (typos, dates) are fixed in place. Substantive errors (facts, figures, names) are corrected and a note is added at the top or bottom of the article stating what was changed and when.

How to report an error

Email editor@mindnewsng.com with the article URL and a description of the error. We acknowledge within 48 hours.

Removal requests

We do not remove accurate reporting on request. We will consider corrections, clarifications and right-of-reply additions.
